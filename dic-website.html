Q
Who are we?我们是谁

NYUSHDIC 是科技爱好者不可错过的社群。我们来自上海纽约大学 计算机科学、交互媒体艺术、商科与金融、数学、数据科学等专业，用不同的学科视角， 共同追问同一个问题：人工智能能为现实世界解决什么？

从一群学生自发创办，到如今连续举办五届的旗舰赛事，我们始终相信一件事—— 多元让创新更有力量。不同的专业背景，让我们的赛制设计、评审标准 更全面；而不同的地域与文化背景，则让每一次比赛都成为真正的思想碰撞。

我们从不把自己局限在一所学校、一座城市。去年，来自六个国家、数十所高校的选手 与我们并肩创作。我们想把 DIC 建成一个跨区域的创新舞台： 以上海纽约大学为起点，辐射整个长三角，也向更远的地方敞开。

We're a community of NYU Shanghai students spanning Computer Science, Interactive Media Arts, Business & Finance, Mathematics and Data Science — asking one shared question: what can AI actually solve in the real world? Diversity is our default setting. Different disciplines make our competition design sharper; different regions and cultures make every edition a genuine collision of ideas. We're building DIC into a cross-regional stage — rooted at NYU Shanghai, reaching across the Yangtze River Delta, and open to far beyond.

Q
What do we do?我们做什么

每一年，DIC 的重头戏都是数字化创新大赛（Digital Innovation Challenge）—— 一项以技术驱动的创新赛事，鼓励跨学科团队运用前沿数字技术探索现实问题的解决方案， 以技术回馈社会。

Every year, the heart of DIC is the Digital Innovation Challenge — a technology-driven competition that invites interdisciplinary teams to tackle real-world problems with cutting-edge digital tools, and to give back to society through what they build.

刚刚落幕的第五届大赛以 "AI Agent Unlock Business Innovation" （AI 智能体解锁商业创新）为主题，聚焦具备感知、推理、自主行动与持续学习能力的 AI Agents， 探讨它们在商业与社会中的真实应用。本届大赛由上纽大 DIC 学生社团主办，获上纽大 计算机科学、数据科学与工程学部及数据科学中心全程指导， 并与多家行业伙伴携手。

Our just-concluded 5th edition, themed "AI Agent Unlock Business Innovation," focused on AI agents that perceive, reason, act autonomously and keep learning — and how they create real value in business and society. It was hosted by the DIC student club, guided throughout by NYU Shanghai's Computer Science, Data Science and Engineering faculty and its Data Science center, and powered by industry partners.

01三大赛道Three Tracks

大赛设立教育、医疗、金融三大赛道，要求团队以人工智能为基础，立足现实需求 提出创新方案，把课堂所学与实践结合，让理论成果真正落地。经过近五个月层层选拔， 九支队伍晋级决赛，在"创新日"（Innovation Day）现场展示成果，最终由评审评出各奖项。

The Challenge runs three tracks — Education, Healthcare and Finance — asking teams to ground AI in real needs, connect classroom learning with practice, and turn theory into something that works. After nearly five months of selection, nine finalist teams presented on Innovation Day, where judges chose the awards.

02行业伙伴与评审Partners & Judges

本届大赛得到腾讯、复宏汉霖（Henlius）、躺着学（ChillPrep）等伙伴的支持。 决赛评审团汇聚商业、艺术、教育与科技等领域的专家学者，从创新性、实用价值与整体表现 等多个维度综合评估——让学生的方案接受真实世界标准的检验。

This edition was supported by partners including Tencent, Henlius and ChillPrep. Our finals jury brought together experts from business, art, education and technology, evaluating projects on innovation, practical value and overall execution — holding student work to real-world standards.

03一路走来的活动The Road There

DIC 从来不只是一场比赛，而是一整套成长平台。过去一年，组委会通过企业参访、 嘉宾讲座、实践工作坊等多种形式，支持学生把想法一步步落地，并为每支队伍配备 导师，在项目推进中提供专业指导。

DIC has never been just a competition — it's a full growth platform. Over the past year, the organizing committee ran company visits, guest talks and hands-on workshops, and paired every team with mentors for guidance as their projects took shape.

我们请来 Sancus Ventures 合伙人杜磊，与二十多位同学面对面聊连续创业与投资；也办过 原创的 "Idea-to-App with base44" 与 Vibe Coding 工作坊—— 让参与者现场把一个念头，变成一个能跑起来的应用原型。

We hosted Du Lei, Partner at Sancus Ventures, for a candid conversation with 20+ students on serial entrepreneurship and investing — and ran our original "Idea-to-App with base44" and Vibe Coding workshops, where participants turned a single idea into a working app prototype on the spot.
